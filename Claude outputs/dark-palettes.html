--ink #ededed --paper #1a1a1a --shell #0d0d0d --accent #e0c060 --muted #9a9a9a --rule #2b2b2b

To use one: copy its six values into your [data-theme="dark"] block. The variable names never change — only the values — so nothing else in your stylesheet needs touching.

Two rules if you'd rather invent your own: keep --paper slightly lighter than --shell, so cards still look like they sit on top of the page — the same relationship as your light theme. And lighten the accent: a colour that reads well on cream looks muddy on near-black, which is why #7a5c3e becomes something like #c9a77c.

Avoid pure white text on pure black. It glares and causes halation on most screens. Every option here uses an off-white around #e8–#f2 for exactly that reason — worth one sentence in your technical documentation.
